Default DEM terrain imagery in this repository is generated on demand from the
Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) seamless photo tile endpoint:

- https://cyberjapandata.gsi.go.jp/xyz/seamlessphoto/{z}/{x}/{y}.jpg

Official reference pages:

- GSI tile list: https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/
- GSI Maps terms of use: https://maps.gsi.go.jp/help/termsofuse.html
- Seamless photo caution / source note PDF:
  https://cyberjapandata.gsi.go.jp/legend/seamlessphoto_precaution.pdf

Current repository behavior:

- The default URL template is defined in
  src/Plateau.ResoniteLink.Application/Importing/LocalCityGmlResonitePlanBuilder.cs
  as `DefaultDemTerrainTextureUrlTemplate`.
- The imagery is not checked into this repository as a bundled asset.
- The CLI downloads tiles at build time only when DEM terrain texture overlays
  are generated.

Usage and attribution note:

- GSI Maps terms state that tile usage follows the GSI content terms and that
  some tiles may include third-party rights or other legal restrictions.
- The `seamlessphoto` tile entry identifies the source as `全国最新写真（シームレス）`
  and points to the official caution PDF for source composition details.
- Some seamless photo coverage uses orthophotos provided by local governments or
  other parties. Those areas can require additional attribution or separate
  approval for further reproduction or use.

Before publishing derived content or redistributing generated terrain imagery,
review the current official GSI pages above for the exact tile coverage being
used.
