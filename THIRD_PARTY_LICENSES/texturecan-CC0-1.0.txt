Ingest procedure:
- Download the 2K Maps archive from the source asset page.
- Run `dotnet run --project tools/DefaultMaterialIngest/DefaultMaterialIngest.csproj -- texturecan <asset-id> <zip-path> <destination-directory>`.
- The ingest tool keeps color, OpenGL normal, height, and emission maps when present.
- The ingest tool generates `<asset-id>_2K_Metallic.png` as a packed material map. Red is upstream metallic when present or neutral 0, green is upstream ambient occlusion or neutral 255, blue preserves upstream roughness or neutral 255 for diagnostics, and alpha is Unity-style smoothness (`255 - roughness`) for linear data sampling.
